Our Story

From JL Netfin to
JL Media Vision.

JL Media Vision comes from the same place as JL Influence: thirty years around athletes, coaches and families, and a lot of game days spent hunting for the right channel.

It started as JL Netfin, an IPTV app that ran on rented servers and a stack of moving parts that had to be kept alive every Saturday and Sunday. It worked, and it showed what this could be.

JL Media Vision is that idea rebuilt from the ground up. No servers to babysit. Your providers, your keys, your profiles, and your teams front and center on every screen.

— Coach Jafar, JL Influence

Privacy

No ads. No tracking. Your provider logins and API keys stay on your device. Your JL account only syncs your profiles and favorites.

Download

Get JL Media Vision.

Desktop

Windows

Windows 10 and 11 · 64-bit · .exe

Download for Windows
  1. Open the installer from your Downloads.
  2. If Windows warns you, choose More info, then Run anyway.
  3. Sign in and add your sources.

TV

Android TV & Shield

Android TV 7 and newer · .apk

Download for Android TV
  1. Install Downloader from the Play Store.
  2. Enter mediavision.jl-stream.com/tv and press Go.
  3. Choose Install, then Open.

Browser

Web app

Chrome, Edge, Safari or Firefox

Open in your browser
  1. Open the web app and sign in.
  2. Use your browser's Install app option to keep it handy.
  3. The desktop app adds HDR, surround sound and smoother seeking.

FAQ

Questions.

Does JL Media Vision come with channels or movies?

No. JL Media Vision is the player. You bring your own IPTV provider, debrid service and addons.

Is it free?

Yes. Your provider and debrid services bill you separately.

Do I need a JL account?

Signing in keeps your profiles, favorite teams and players the same on every device you use.

How do I update?

Download and install again from this page. Your settings and sign-in stay as they are.

A JL Influence product.

JL Media Vision is meant for personal use with sources you have the legal right to use.

JL Media Vision hosts no media, runs no public index of media sources and ships no content. It is a player for services you choose to connect. Use only services and sources that permit your access, and follow their terms and the laws where you live. Product names belong to their owners.
