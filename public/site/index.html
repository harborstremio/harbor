Questions

Does JL Media Vision come with channels or movies?

No. JL Media Vision is the player. You bring your own IPTV provider, debrid service and addons, and JL Media Vision brings them together in one place.

What do I need to get started?

An M3U playlist link or Xtream login from your provider for live TV. For movies and shows, a Real-Debrid or TorBox account and the Torrentio addon. Setup walks you through each step, and every step is optional.

Is it free?

Yes, the app is free to download and use. Your provider and debrid services bill you separately.

Why do I sign in?

Your JL account keeps profiles, favorite teams and players in sync, so your desktop, browser and TV all show the same thing. Provider passwords and API keys stay on your device.

Why does Windows warn me when I install it?

New apps that haven't built up a download history with Microsoft get that warning. Choose More info, then Run anyway.
