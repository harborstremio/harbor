  1. 1
    Download the installer

    JL-Media-Vision-Setup-x64.exe saves to your Downloads folder.

    Run it

    If Windows shows "Windows protected your PC", choose More info, then Run anyway.

  3. 3
    Sign in and add your sources

    Setup walks you through your IPTV playlist, Real-Debrid or TorBox, and Torrentio. Every step is optional.

Questions

Does it come with channels or movies?

No. JL Media Vision is the player. You bring your own IPTV provider, debrid service and addons.

Is it free?

Yes. Your provider and debrid services bill you separately.

Why does Windows warn me?

New apps without a download history get that warning. Choose More info, then Run anyway.

How do I update?

Download and install again from this page. Your settings and sign-in stay as they are.
