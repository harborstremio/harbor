# Settings preview artwork

Sources for the film footage, posters and music artwork in Harbor's settings previews. Film records identify public-domain status in the United States and applicable source-country status. The Library of Congress dates the music cover to 1911.

| Local asset | Work and source |
| --- | --- |
| `harbor-discover.webp` | Harbor Discover screenshot, with media artwork from Steamboat Willie and The General. Film sources are listed below. |
| `steamboat-willie.webp`, `steamboat-river.webp`, `steamboat-deck.webp` | **Steamboat Willie (1928)**, Walt Disney and Ub Iwerks. Frames at approximately 00:35, 00:25 and 01:00 from the [Internet Archive film](https://archive.org/details/SteamboatWillie). [Wikimedia Commons film and public-domain record](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Steamboat_Willie_(1928)_by_Walt_Disney.webm). Source pillarboxing removed; lossless WebP. |
| `the-general-still.webp` | **The General (1926)**, Buster Keaton and Clyde Bruckman. [Original still and rights record](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:The_General,_front.jpg). Resized and converted to WebP. |
| `the-general-poster.webp` | **The General (1926)**, original theatrical artwork by Alvan “Hap” Hadley (signed). [Original poster and rights record](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:The_general_movie_poster.jpg). Resized and converted to WebP. |
| `sherlock-jr-poster.webp` | **Sherlock Jr. (1924)**. [Original poster and rights record](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Sherlock_jr_poster.jpg). Resized and converted to WebP. |

| `the-toll-of-the-sea.webp` | **The Toll of the Sea (1922)**. Anna May Wong, Priscilla Moran and Beatrice Bentley; cinematography J. A. Ball, editing Hal C. Kern, distributed by Metro Pictures. The subtitle text, episode labels and badge values in settings are illustrative controls, not quotations or factual metadata for these films. Existing custom controller drawings, service logos, theme screenshots and user-uploaded images are separate assets and retain their existing provenance.
